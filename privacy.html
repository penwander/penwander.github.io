Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Penwander is a free tool that turns images into plotter-style line drawings. It is built so that your images stay on your own device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • Your images are processed entirely in your browser and are never uploaded to Penwander or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
  • Your settings and Trace drawings are saved only in your own browser.

Images you open

When you open, paste or drop an image, it is read and processed by code running in your browser. Penwander has no server that receives images, so the images and the drawings made from them are never sent anywhere. Files you export (PNG, SVG or TXT) are created on your device and saved wherever you choose.

What is saved on your device

To pick up where you left off, Penwander saves your control settings and your Trace & draw strokes in your browser’s local storage. The original image is not saved there. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Services Penwander relies on

Two outside services are involved in delivering the page. Each receives the ordinary technical information any website request includes, such as your IP address and browser type, under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Penwander does not receive or have access to this information.

Children

Penwander does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue on Penwander’s GitHub page.
